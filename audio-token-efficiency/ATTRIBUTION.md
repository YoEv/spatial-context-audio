# Audio Representation Lab

This report presents the completed 2026-09-28 RTX 3060 experiment. Its aggregate
results are exported from the recorded runs; the page does not run new models.

The three listening examples derive from **Speech Commands v0.02**, by Pete
Warden / Google, licensed under **CC BY 4.0**. Source digit clips were normalized,
placed on a shared timeline, gain-adjusted and mixed. PCM16 playback copies are
used here. The separate sources and A–B–mixture walkthrough are explanatory aids;
the mixture experiments received only the 3.8-second mono mixture.

- Dataset: https://storage.googleapis.com/download.tensorflow.org/data/speech_commands_v0.02.tar.gz
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03209
- License: [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE-Speech-Commands.txt)

The examples were selected by FireRed seed-0 outcomes for illustration, not at
random. Their predictions are recorded test outputs at M=8, seed=0. They do not
estimate the overall accuracy. No model weights, complete dataset, private host
paths, credentials or full per-example prediction files are published here.

Architecture explanations are paraphrases linked to the original papers on the
page. Diagrams are original explanatory schematics, not copied paper figures.
